<h2 id="create-share-link">Create a resume sharing link</h2>

After publishing a resume, choose a background, top area, and page style for its public view, then generate a link that is ready to share.

[Customize a public resume](/docs/public-resume-customization)

<h2 id="preview-and-share">Preview before sharing</h2>

- Open the preview first and check the background, top area, and resume position.
- Appearance choices apply only to the sharing link. They do not change the resume in the editor or its PDF.
- Whether a public page offers PDF download depends on the platform's public-download setting.
