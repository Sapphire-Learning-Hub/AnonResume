<h2 id="profile-password">Profile and password</h2>

Use the account center to change your display name or password. To update the password, enter the current one and choose a new password that you do not reuse on other sites. After the change, use the new password when signing in on other devices.

<h2 id="change-email">Change the sign-in email</h2>

Changing email has three steps: verify the current password, verify the current email, and verify the new email. Complete each step before continuing. Once the new address is verified, it becomes the sign-in email.

If a code does not arrive, check spam, confirm the address, and wait for the resend countdown. Never share a verification code with anyone.

<h2 id="login-devices">Review signed-in devices</h2>

**Login devices** lists current active sign-ins. If you see a device you do not recognize or no longer use, remove it and change your password immediately. Removing the current device ends the current sign-in.

<h2 id="close-account">Close the account</h2>

Before requesting deletion, export your personal data and complete password and email verification. The account then enters a seven-day cooling-off period and cannot be used normally. You can cancel deletion from the page during this period, but password recovery is unavailable.

After seven days the account is closed. Make sure you have saved anything you need and unpublished resumes that should no longer be public before submitting the request.
