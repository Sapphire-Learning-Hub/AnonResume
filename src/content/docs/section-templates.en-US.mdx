<h2 id="choose-template">Choose and preview a template</h2>

Open **Insert** in the editor and choose **Choose section template…**. The template list explains what each section is designed to hold. Hover over a template and select **Preview** to inspect its structure and example before inserting it.

<h2 id="insert-edit">Insert and edit a section</h2>

Selecting **Insert** adds the section to the current resume. A template is only a starting point, so replace all example content with your own information.

- Rename the section to match your experience and preferred wording.
- Remove example entries that do not apply and add the details you need.
- Use layout ordering to move the section into the right position.

<h2 id="templates-presets">Section templates and visual presets</h2>

A section template adds an editable content structure, such as a work experience or project entry. A visual preset changes fonts, colors, and layout across the resume without adding experience content.

Use a visual preset under **Design** when you only want a different look. Use a section template when you need a new content structure.
