<h2 id="get-started">Get started</h2>

Use the link builder below. You do not need to edit the URL manually:

1. Sign in and select a published resume.
2. Adjust the page colors, top area, resume page style, and position.
3. Open the preview, confirm the result, and copy the sharing link.

<h2 id="adjust-appearance">Customize the page</h2>

- **Colors and background**: choose light, dark, or automatic colors with a soft gradient or solid background.
- **Top area**: show the title and actions, keep only the title, or hide the area entirely.
- **Resume page style**: use a shadow, thin border, or no frame, then choose where the resume sits on the page.
- **Size and spacing**: adjust the space around the resume, its display width, and the distance between pages.

For a custom color, enter a familiar six-character color value such as `FFFFFF`. The field already includes `#`.

<h2 id="preview-and-share">Preview and share</h2>

Select **Open preview** to check the finished page, then copy the link into a portfolio, personal site, or recruiting system. Appearance choices do not alter resume content or its PDF. The platform setting controls whether the public page shows a download button.
