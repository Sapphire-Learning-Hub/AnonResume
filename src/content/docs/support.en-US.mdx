<h2 id="before-contacting">Before contacting support</h2>

Start with [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting), refresh the page, and confirm that the problem can be reproduced. If it affects only one resume, note the resume name, but do not send the complete resume through a public channel.

<h2 id="prepare-feedback">Prepare useful feedback</h2>

A report is easier to investigate when it includes:

- The approximate time and type of device used.
- The steps from opening the page to seeing the problem.
- What you expected and what happened instead.
- Recovery steps you already tried.
- A screenshot or recording with sensitive details hidden.

<h2 id="protect-information">Protect personal information</h2>

Never submit passwords, email verification codes, API keys, or an unredacted resume. Before taking a screenshot, hide names, contact details, identity information, and any other personal content that is not needed.
