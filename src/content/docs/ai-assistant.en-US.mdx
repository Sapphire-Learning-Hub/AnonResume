<h2 id="start-request">Start a request</h2>

Open **AI** in the editor, choose an available service, and describe both the content and the outcome you want. A request such as “shorten this project entry while keeping the measured results” is more useful than “make this better.”

Submit only the content needed for the task. Never include passwords, email verification codes, API keys, or unrelated personal information.

<h2 id="review-result">Review before applying</h2>

Choose “Quality review” from the editor’s AI menu to check the saved resume for completeness, clarity, experience and outcomes, organization, and consistency. Leave the target role and job description blank for a general review, or enter them to add role-specific analysis. With a job description, the report compares its requirements with evidence in your resume.

Scores help prioritize improvements. A role title alone produces qualitative feedback; a description with enough explicit requirements also produces a separate content-match score. These scores do not predict hiring outcomes or prevent publishing and exporting.

Locate the quoted content, ignore inapplicable findings, or select findings to generate editable suggestions. Supply real facts when an improvement needs missing experience or outcomes. Reviews and edit suggestions are separate requests, each billed according to actual usage of the selected service.

Past reports remain available for the resume. Content edits mark older reports as out of date; you can request another review when needed. Appearance changes such as colors and font sizes do not invalidate a content review.

Quality reviews read the resume, evaluate its dimensions, and assemble the report in steps. You can follow progress or stop at any time. Temporary AI service timeouts trigger a limited number of retries; if the review still cannot finish, the page and past report show the reason. Retries may also incur service usage. Closing the panel or refreshing does not create a new task. If a task is interrupted, you must start another review yourself.

AI suggestions can omit facts, change the intended tone, or introduce inaccurate details. Before applying a result, check names, dates, numbers, skills, and experience. Make sure the final content is truthful, accurate, and still sounds like you.

If a suggestion is not suitable, revise the request and try again, or use only the useful parts. You remain in control of the final content.

<h2 id="request-problems">When a request does not finish</h2>

- Check your connection and try once more after a short wait.
- Submit less content at a time, or split a complex request into clear steps.
- If the page says no service is available, contact the instance administrator.
- If repeated attempts fail, note the time, steps, and message shown on the page before opening **Get support**.
