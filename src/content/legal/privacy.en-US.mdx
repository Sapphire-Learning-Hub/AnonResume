## Scope of this policy

This policy explains how the current AnonResume instance handles information while providing accounts, resume editing, publishing, export, and support. The operator shown above is responsible for this instance. If another organization provides the instance you use, rely on the information displayed by that instance.

## Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, the instance may handle:

- Account details such as sign-in email, display name, and account status.
- Resumes, sections, links, images, and layout settings that you create or import.
- Publishing status, public links, and information needed to complete exports.
- Signed-in devices, active sessions, and verification records used to protect the account.
- Necessary records used to operate the service, investigate failures, and respond to feedback.
- Content you deliberately submit to the AI assistant and the settings needed to complete that request.

## How information is used

Information is used to create and save resumes, synchronize edits, produce previews and PDFs, publish or withdraw public pages, protect accounts, provide AI features you choose to use, and respond to failures or support requests.

Drafts are not made public merely because a feature is enabled. A public page is created only when you publish a resume.

## Public resumes and sharing links

Anyone who receives a public link may be able to open it. Review the resume before sharing and remove anything that should not be public. Unpublishing stops this instance from serving the public page, but it cannot remove copies that other people already downloaded, captured, or saved.

Public-page appearance settings change presentation only. They do not alter the resume content or its PDF.

## AI and external services

When you deliberately use the AI assistant, content in the request may be sent to the model service selected by you or provided by the instance. Remove unrelated personal information before submitting, and verify facts, numbers, and wording before applying a suggestion.

The instance may also use email delivery, third-party sign-in, storage, or other infrastructure services to complete features you request. Those services receive only the information needed for their role and may apply their own policies.

## Retention, export, and account deletion

Information is retained while needed to provide account and resume features. The account center lets you export an Excel file for personal reference.

After a deletion request, the account enters a seven-day cooling-off period. You can cancel during that period. When it ends, the account is closed and normal access stops. Some records may be retained for a reasonable period to protect the service, maintain security, or meet necessary responsibilities, but they are not used to restore normal access to a closed account.

## Information you can manage

Within the features provided by the product, you can:

- Review and correct profile details and resume content.
- Change the sign-in email or password and remove devices you no longer use.
- Publish, update, or withdraw a public resume.
- Export personal data and request account deletion.
- Ask the current instance operator how information is handled using the contact details above.

## Security, children, and policy updates

Protect passwords and email verification codes, and remove devices you do not recognize. If someone else may have accessed the account, change the password immediately and contact support.

The service is not intended for children who cannot independently consent to using an online service. Obtain guardian consent first where it is required.

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The revised effective date will appear on this page. Use the contact details or support link above if you have questions.
